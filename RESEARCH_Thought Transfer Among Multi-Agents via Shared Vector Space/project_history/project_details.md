# Project: THOUGHTCOMM (Reimplementation and Research Exploration)

## Overview
- **Goal**: To understand, reconstruct, validate, and extend the ideas proposed in the THOUGHTCOMM paper from first principles. The codebase must be highly modular, readable, reproducible, and research-friendly rather than just focusing on benchmark numbers. Success means every module is independently understandable and a new contributor could easily extend it.
- **Stack**: Python 3.11+, PyTorch, Hugging Face transformers, Git/GitHub, VS Code, Google Colab
- **Started**: 2026-08-06
- **Project Status**: ACTIVE

## Constraints
- Must remain executable on a Google Colab T4 GPU.
- LLM weights must remain frozen; only train the Sparse Autoencoder and Adapter.
- All logic must be in Python modules, not Jupyter notebooks (notebooks are for orchestration only).
- Strict adherence to PEP 8, type hinting, and modularity.
